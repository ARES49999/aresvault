Ares Vault Usage & Architecture

Ares Vault uses personal cognitive seeds to synthesize deterministic, high-entropy passwords that are easy for you to recall but computationally infeasible to brute force.

Step 01

Define Cognitive Anchors

Supply parameters you inherently remember: your name or handle, birth date (from which year, month, and day are derived), a favorite color, and a memorable anchor word or token.

Step 02

Deterministic Synthesis

The engine applies character substitution matrices (leetspeak), boundary framing, case rhythm, and optional cryptographic salt to expand candidate search spaces past 80-95 bits.

Step 03

Select Credential Archetype

Pick the format suited for your target platform: Fortress Tactical for primary master accounts, Memorable Passphrase for mobile typing, or Alphanumeric PIN for strict banking portals.

100% Client-Side Memory Sandbox

All inputs, computations, and entropy calculations execute strictly in your local browser runtime via the W3C Web Cryptography API. No network requests are made, no telemetry is transmitted, and no state is cached to local storage. You can disconnect your device from the internet and Ares Vault will continue functioning without interruption.
