Ares Vault Usage & Security Guide

How Ares Vault translates memorable personal anchors into cryptographically resilient passwords without storing any data.

1

Provide Your Anchors

Enter details that are naturally easy for you to remember: your name or handle, birth date (extracting year, month, day), a favorite color, and a memorable custom word or symbol.

2

Cryptographic Synthesis

Ares Vault runs client-side transformation algorithms to inject leetspeak substitutions, interleaving boundaries, and high-entropy salt to prevent dictionary and rainbow-table attacks.

3

Choose Your Archetype

Select between Fortress Tactical (for primary credentials), Memorable Passphrases (for mobile and daily typing), or Alphanumeric PINs (for legacy systems with symbol limits).

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Ares Vault processes all inputs in your local browser sandbox memory using Web Crypto APIs. No personal data, passwords, analytics, cookies, or telemetry are ever recorded, cached to disk, or transmitted across any network. You can disconnect your internet and Ares Vault will continue operating with full functionality.
